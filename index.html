HOW TO PLAY

ENEMIES WALK DOWN THE BLACK PATH.
STOP THEM REACHING THE BOTTOM.

AN ENEMY'S COLOUR IS ITS HEALTH.
EACH OF RED, GREEN, BLUE GOES FROM 255 TO 0. BLACK = DEAD.

EACH TOWER DRAINS ONE COLOUR.
A RED TOWER CAN'T HURT A GREEN ENEMY. BUILD THE RIGHT MIX.

TAP A TILE TO PREVIEW A TOWER.
TAP THE SAME TILE AGAIN TO BUILD IT.

SHOOTERS FIRE BULLETS.
BIG HITS, LONGER RANGE, CAN WASTE DAMAGE.

BEAMS DRAIN NONSTOP.
UNLOCK EACH COLOUR'S BEAM WITH XP. NO WASTE, SHORTER RANGE.

DRAINING COLOUR EARNS COIN.
TAP A BUILT TOWER TO SEE ITS RANGE OR SELL IT.

EVERY ATTEMPT EARNS XP.
SPEND IT ON UPGRADES BETWEEN RUNS.
